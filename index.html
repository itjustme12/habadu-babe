A LITTLE NOTE FOR YOUR SPECIAL DAY

To my babe,
my Ailec, my Lec.

Happy birthday, babe. ♡

I honestly don't even know how to start this because how do you put someone who means so much to you into words? I could write the longest letter ever and still feel like I haven't said enough about how grateful I am to have you in my life.

Lec, you are genuinely one of those people who make life feel a little less heavy. You're funny, outgoing, makulit, and full of life. You can turn the most random conversation into something we'll probably laugh about for days. Sometimes, I wonder if you even realize how much happiness you bring just by being yourself. You don't have to do anything extraordinary. Sometimes, just having you around is already enough.

But babe, beyond the jokes, the laughter, and the version of you that everyone gets to see, I hope you always have a place where you can be completely yourself. A place where you don't have to be funny when you're sad, strong when you're tired, or okay when you're really not. You deserve to be cared for on your quiet days, too. You deserve love that doesn't depend on how much happiness you give everyone else.

I'm so thankful that out of all the people in this world, I got to meet you. Somewhere between all the random talks, silly moments, and memories, you became someone so important to me. You're not just someone I can laugh with. You're someone whose happiness matters to me, someone I want to see succeed, and someone I genuinely hope life treats kindly.

I know we can't keep everything the same forever. We'll grow, change, get busy, and have our own struggles to figure out. There will be days when life doesn't go the way we planned. But I hope that even as life changes, you never forget how special you are to me. I hope I get to witness more of your birthdays, celebrate your little wins, hear about your big dreams, and laugh with you about the things that only we understand.

On your birthday, I wish you the kind of happiness that stays even after the candles are blown out. I wish you peaceful days, genuine friendships, answered prayers, exciting opportunities, and reasons to believe in yourself even when things get difficult. I hope you become everything you've been dreaming of becoming, without ever feeling that you have to lose the beautiful parts of who you are along the way.

And please, babe, be kind to yourself. You don't have to have your whole life figured out. You don't have to be perfect. You don't have to prove that you're worth loving. I hope you learn to see the goodness in yourself that the people who love you can already see. I hope this year gives you more reasons to be proud of your journey, even when progress feels small.

Thank you for existing, Lec. Thank you for being your funny, chaotic, lovable self. Thank you for the memories we've made and for all the ordinary moments that became special because you were part of them. I may not always know how to express how much you mean to me, but I hope you never have to question whether you matter in my life.

If there's one thing I want you to remember from this letter, it's that you are loved for more than the laughs you give people or the happiness you bring into a room. You are loved simply because you're you. And I'm so, so glad that the world has you in it.

Happy birthday again, my babe. Here's to more memories, more chika, more uncontrollable laughter, more dreams coming true, and more years of us being part of each other's lives. Whatever the future looks like, I'll always be wishing good things for you.

I love you so much, Ailec. More than this little website could ever explain. If I could give you anything today, I'd give you a way to see yourself through the eyes of someone who loves you, even just for a moment. Maybe then you'd understand why I'm so thankful for you.

with all my love,

your person, always. ♡
